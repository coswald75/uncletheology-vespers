Study this song

Teaching aids drawn from the song — for personal study or group discussion.

How the song moves

1
Born again = new ontology
Whoever trusts has been born again; “divine biology,” life hooked to eternity, security in perpetuity (1 John 5:1; John 3:3).
2
Same voice, two books
John 17’s high-priestly prayer and the farewell discourse re-lit in the epistle: “love one another” proves who’s in (John 13:34–35; 17).
3
The test of life
Walking in light, keeping His word, no fear in love — fruit, not lip-service optics (1 John 1:5–7; 4:18).
4
Kept to the end
“I’m His, and He cannot deny Himself”; abiding now builds confidence at His appearing (2 Timothy 2:13; 1 John 2:28).

Key terms & allusions

  • ontology / divine biology — The song’s repeated claim that the new birth is a change in being, not behavior — you act because you are.
  • the farewell discourse — Jesus’ upper-room teaching, John 13–17, the source of “abide in My love” and the Counselor’s coming.
  • “I’m His, and He cannot deny Himself” — Assurance grounded in God’s faithfulness rather than ours — 2 Timothy 2:13, “if we are faithless, He remains faithful.”
  • “no fear in love” — 1 John 4:18 — “perfect love casts out fear.”
  • the dove — The Spirit/Counselor John 15 promises, who “keeps us from runnin’.”

Discussion questions

  1. John gives “tests of life” (light, love, belief). How can a community use them for assurance without turning them into anxiety?
  2. “Love one another — proves who is in and who ain’t.” How does that challenge the way we measure spiritual maturity?
  3. What’s the difference between assurance rooted in “He cannot deny Himself” and assurance rooted in our own performance?

For contemplation

  1. Do you relate to God as someone trying to become His, or someone who already is His?
  2. Where does “no fear in love” need to replace fear in your walk?
  3. What would change if you truly believed your security is “in perpetuity”?
