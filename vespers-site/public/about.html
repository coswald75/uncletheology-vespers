About

The last hour of your day

Vespers is a webcast for the tail end of the day — the hour when the noise finally quiets and there's room to think about something big.

Each episode takes up one big idea and stays with it. On some nights we work through Scripture, and on other nights, an entry in Mortimer Adler's Syntopicon, and others from the public bookshelf of our local libraries.

The form is simple — mostly talk, with just a little jazz stitched between the segments, a nocturne to change the light before the next movement begins.

Let us take up the last hour of your day.
